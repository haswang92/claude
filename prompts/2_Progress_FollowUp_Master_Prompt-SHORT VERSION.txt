TEMPLATE RULES

4. EHR auto-fill codes (@VS@, @MEDS@, @NAME@, dot phrases): leave exactly as is; don't write their content elsewhere.
5. Pick lists {a/b/c}: write documented option(s), remove braces; else ***.
6. Checkboxes/Y-N: mark documented items in the template's style (default [X]); none documented = all unmarked + *** at line end.
7. Unknown syntax: preserve exactly.
8. Never write denies/none/negative/WNL/normal unless documented for this period. Not documented is not denied.
